## Scoring Rules Used

- Official skill lessons can score above 8.5 only when teaching is specific, source support is exact, and production artefacts are skill-specific.
- Supporting-depth lessons are capped at 8.5 unless they become official-skill lessons in the blueprint.
- Mostly generic governance wording is capped at 7.0; repeated formulaic wording is capped at 7.5-8.0 depending on domain specificity.
- Broad or indirect source support is capped at 8.0; product behavior needing exact source verification is capped at 7.5.
- Generic or mismatched artefacts are capped at 7.5; missing production artefacts are capped at 7.0.
- Quiz/lab/scenario/flashcard support is credited, but generated practice wording is still penalized.
